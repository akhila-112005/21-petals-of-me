The idea behind 21 Petals

Not just flowers.

Every flower in this garden represents something I want to remember while growing through life.

Some flowers represent months. Some represent feelings. Some represent dreams, courage, memories and the little things that make me who I am.

I called it 21 Petals because twenty-one is more than just an age. It is a collection of everything that has grown, changed and stayed with me.

For the girl I will become

A Letter to My Future Self

Dear Future Me,

If you're reading this, another year has passed.

Maybe everything happened exactly as you hoped. Maybe it didn't.

Maybe some flowers bloomed early, some bloomed late, and some are still waiting for their season.

That's okay.

You were never meant to bloom all at once.

You are allowed to grow slowly. You are allowed to change your dreams. You are allowed to begin again. You are allowed to become someone you haven't imagined yet.

So wherever you are when you read this —

Keep going.

There are still mornings you haven't seen, people you haven't met, places you haven't visited, songs you haven't heard, and flowers you haven't stopped to notice.

Your story is still blooming.

— A little letter from Akhila's flower garden
